About

Naavya Sethi

Naavya is a designer and computer scientist working where systems meet sensation. She explores how large datasets — restaurant networks, delivery graphs, community structure — can become visual language: generative art you can read as both analysis and object.

Her data-art practice began with Zomato delivery data from India: embeddings, similarity matching, network analysis, and community discovery rendered as paper-spinners, radial forms, and circular meshes. Art she loved as a child; code she learned to build with; the two, finally, in the same frame.

When she isn’t shaping graphs into color, she’s usually sketching, reading, or chasing the next way to make the invisible feel tangible. If you’d like to learn more, feel free to get in touch.
