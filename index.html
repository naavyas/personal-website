Project 01

Generative Data Art

The paper

Network Analysis and Generation of Data Art from Restaurants Data

This all grew out of a co-authored paper on Bangalore's restaurant scene. We pulled Zomato data - cuisines, menus, featured dishes, reviews - and built a similarity network across 8,792 restaurants: TF-IDF embeddings per place, then nearest-neighbour matching on the twenty closest, quantile-weighted into 147,424 edges.

Community detection ran four ways - Label Propagation, Louvain with a Constant Potts Model, Demon, and Bayesian statistical inference. Demon read best: it grouped restaurants by region - Tamil Nadu, Bihar, the Delhi-Punjab-Haryana belt - with no location data in the graph at all.

A bar chart would have told you the degree distribution follows a power law. It would not have shown which communities sit next to each other, or which restaurant is quietly bridging two cuisines it has no business bridging. So we turned the analysis into graphs.
